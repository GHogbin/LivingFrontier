Living Frontier 0.2.0-beta.2 - Exploration Beta

Requires Minecraft Java 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.0 and Java 17.
No additional mod dependencies.

Adds roaming wild boars, hostile forest prowler packs and flying sky
wraiths with telegraphed diving attacks. Includes original animated
models/textures, new spawn eggs, biome spawning and loot.

Adds occasional hostile exploration encounters: daylight raider bands
and nighttime prowler/wraith groups, with warnings, saved 6-12 minute
cooldowns, movement requirements and configurable local/global caps.
Protects Creative/Spectator players, villages, guarded bases, lit ground
and unsuitable spawning locations. No forced chunk generation.

Keeps all Beta 1 wildlife, guarded camps, ruined keeps and the Warlord.
Corrects daylight raider spawning and permits forest-canopy patrols.

Replace the Beta 1 JAR with this JAR and restart the Forge instance.
Do not install both versions. Back up worlds before upgrading.
Eight spawn eggs are available; new mobs can spawn in existing 1.20.1 saves.

Beta limitations: hands-on client, multiplayer and balance playtesting
remains necessary. Glowing mobs do not provide dynamic world lighting.
Read START-HERE.txt for configuration, commands and installation details.
