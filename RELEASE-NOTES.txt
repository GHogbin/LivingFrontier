Living Frontier 0.2.0-beta.8 - Wilderness Trade Network Beta

Requires Minecraft Java 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.0 and Java 17.
No additional mod dependencies.

Adds all four village design elements together: biome-specific materials,
medieval defenses, rustic homes/farms, and a fantasy landmark/market square.
Five original 41x41 fortified town centres cover the normal village biomes:
  Plains: oak, white walls and a lantern bell tower.
  Desert: sandstone courtyard homes and an oasis sun obelisk.
  Savanna: acacia, terracotta and an amber wind spire.
  Taiga: spruce, mossy stone and a forest rune shrine.
  Snowy: warm timber homes, covered lit farms and a frost lantern beacon.

Each core has four open road gateways, a defensive perimeter, two climbable
watchtowers, two cottages/four beds, two irrigated farms, four canopy stalls,
vanilla job sites, house loot and a meeting bell. The surrounding village
continues using vanilla roads, homes, professions and normal trading.
The wall protects the core, not every outer building; these are not raids
or puzzle dungeons. Landmarks are decorative, not magic systems.

Vanilla villagers, iron golems, cats and desert camels use their existing
generation pools. The rare abandoned village variants keep their vanilla
templates and original relative weights. No vanilla entity is replaced.
Existing guards, wildlife, village errands, camp/keep puzzles and combat
remain. Paved/snowy village grounds accept their guards, visitor and trader.

Beta 8 removes village-to-village road targeting completely. The world seed
now creates independent wilderness crossroads with roads extending in up to
four pseudo-random directions. Roads are three blocks wide, terrain graded,
biome surfaced, structure aware and stop at ocean terrain.

Viable road cells place one small tent camp and one two-house roadside hamlet.
Sites initialize peaceful residents once when reached. A persistent road
trader shuttles between the pair without force-loading chunks.

Every camp, hamlet and newly discovered inhabited village participates in a
saved settlement economy. Traders unload imports, load local surpluses and
carry an eight-slot physical inventory. Scarcity changes emerald prices.
Right-click uses the vanilla merchant screen; player purchases and sales alter
the trader's real cargo. Trade routes, inventories, stocks, import/export
totals and site initialization survive saving and loading.

Only NEWLY generated village starts use the redesign. Existing settlements,
player buildings and saved vanilla structure templates are not rebuilt.
Wilderness roads and roadside sites generate only in new Beta 8 chunks.
Earlier road chunks and already-upgraded village populations are not
retrofitted. No chunk-load replacement or forced regeneration.
The separate older village-life resident/small-stall feature keeps working.

Replace the previous JAR and restart both client/server as applicable.
Back up saves. Explore new chunks or start a new world to see the designs.
Do not install multiple versions or downgrade saves with puzzle blocks.
Higher-priority datapacks/mods replacing the same vanilla town-centre pools
can override these designs; the mod does not rewrite their data at runtime.

Beta limitations: hands-on client/multiplayer/balance playtesting remains
necessary. One centre layout per biome; outer buildings are still vanilla.
